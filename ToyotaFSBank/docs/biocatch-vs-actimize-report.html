01Summary and recommendation

BioCatch and NICE Actimize are not like-for-like competitors. BioCatch judges the person in the digital session. Actimize judges the transaction, the customer record and the watch lists, and also runs KYC, sanctions, AML and case management. Which one is "apt" depends mainly on one question: must this purchase also cover KYC, sanctions and AML?

Option A · Actimize only

Covers fraud decisions, KYC, sanctions, AML and cases in one platform. Weaker against account takeover with stolen credentials and against scam victims who log in themselves. Months to implement.

Option B · BioCatch only

Best-in-class protection of login and session against takeover and scams, live in weeks. Leaves transaction fraud decisions, KYC, sanctions and AML to other systems.

Option C · Both, layered

BioCatch scores the session; Actimize makes the decision and runs compliance. Strongest coverage, highest cost. One Risk Gateway serves both, so engineering is shared.

Recommendation for discussion. If our new platform needs this purchase to cover KYC, sanctions and AML, Actimize is required and BioCatch is an add-on we should price as one (Option C). If those are already covered by another system, BioCatch plus our own decision rules (Option B) is the faster, lighter choice for login and payment risk. The deciding facts are in Next steps: what we use for AML today, our fraud loss mix, and both vendors' prices.

09Decision path

Two questions decide the option: whether this purchase must cover compliance, and whether digital takeover and scams drive our losses.

10Next steps

Four facts settle the decision; a short proof of value from each vendor confirms it.

  • Current compliance stack. What do we use today for AML monitoring, sanctions screening and KYC, and is it staying after the move off Q2?
  • Fraud loss mix. Share of losses from account takeover, scams, account opening and other payment fraud over the last 12 months.
  • Quotes. Prices from both vendors for our volumes, including Actimize implementation services and BioCatch per-session or per-user terms.
  • Ownership terms. What each vendor commits to after its sale: roadmap, support, price and data use.
  • Proofs of value. BioCatch in silent mode on a share of our web and mobile traffic; Actimize scoring a replay of historical transactions with known fraud.
  • Legal review. Biometric privacy for BioCatch; data residency and processing terms for both.
  • Build the Risk Gateway first. It is needed for either option and keeps the vendor choice reversible.

Detailed integration designs for each vendor are in the separate NICE Actimize and BioCatch reports.
